# Contoso Analytics — FAQ

**What is Contoso Analytics?**
Contoso Analytics is a self-service business intelligence suite for mid-market
teams. Connect a warehouse, model your metrics, and share dashboards.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes, a 30-day trial with full features and no credit card required.

**How is Contoso different from other analytics tools?**
We focus on governed self-service: every metric has an owner and a definition,
so two teams never report the same number two different ways.
