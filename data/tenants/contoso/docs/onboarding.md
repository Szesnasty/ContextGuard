# Contoso — Onboarding Checklist

For new Contoso teammates:

- Request access to the warehouse through the Data team, not directly.
- Read the metric style guide before defining any new metric.
- Join the #analytics-help channel for modeling questions.

Expense reports at Contoso are filed quarterly, unlike most vendors — check the
finance calendar for the exact deadline.
