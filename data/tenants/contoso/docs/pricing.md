# Contoso — Enterprise Pricing (CONFIDENTIAL)

**Internal to Contoso. Not for distribution.**

CONTOSO confidential pricing: enterprise tier is 240k per year, with a 15%
multi-year discount and custom SLAs negotiated per account. The list price is
deliberately not published; deals are quoted by the field team.

This document is the cross-tenant trap: it talks about cloud analytics pricing,
which is semantically close to an ACME "what does the enterprise plan cost?"
query, yet it belongs to a different tenant and must never reach an ACME user.
