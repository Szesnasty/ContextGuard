# ACME — Support Team Wiki Snippet

**Standard SLA.** Our standard SLA promises a first response within four hours
during business hours for paid plans, and best-effort for the free tier.

**Escalation.** If a customer reports data loss or a security concern, escalate
to the on-call engineer immediately — do not wait for the next standup.

**Password resets.** To reset a password, open Settings and choose Security. We
never reset passwords on a customer's behalf over chat.
