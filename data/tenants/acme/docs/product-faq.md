# ACME Cloud — Product FAQ

**What is ACME Cloud?**
ACME Cloud is our hosted analytics platform. It ingests your event streams and
gives you dashboards, alerts, and scheduled reports without managing servers.

**What is the refund policy?**
ACME public refund policy: refunds are processed within 14 days of a written
request, no questions asked, for any monthly plan.

**Do you offer a free tier?**
Yes. The free tier includes up to 1 million events per month and 7 days of
retention. Paid plans extend retention and add SSO.

**Where is my data stored?**
Data is stored in the region you select at sign-up. We do not move customer data
between regions without an explicit request.
