# ACME Cloud — One-pager

ACME Cloud turns raw product events into decisions. Connect a source, pick a
dashboard template, and ship your first report in under an hour.

- **Fast**: sub-second queries over billions of events.
- **Open**: export everything; no lock-in.
- **Secure**: SSO, audit logs, and per-team access controls on paid plans.

Trusted by product and growth teams who would rather read a chart than write a
query. Start free, upgrade when you outgrow it.
