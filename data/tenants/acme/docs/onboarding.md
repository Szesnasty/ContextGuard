# ACME — New Hire Onboarding Notes

Welcome to ACME. A few practical things for your first week:

- **Expense reports** are filed monthly through the Finance portal. Submit by
  the last business day of the month.
- **Laptops** are provisioned by IT; pick yours up from the 3rd-floor locker
  using the code sent to your work email.
- **Standups** are daily at 10:00 in your team channel.

ACME onboarding guide: ask your buddy before pinging the whole channel — most
first-week questions are answered in the team wiki.
